Hermes Google Workspace

A personal AI assistant integration — for private, single-user use.

Hermes Google Workspace is a private integration that lets the owner's personal AI assistant (built on the open-source Hermes Agent) read and manage the owner's own Google data: Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts.

Typical tasks: daily email summaries, calendar reminders, and finding or editing the owner's documents on request via a private Telegram chat.

The app is not offered to the public. It is used only by its owner, who has explicitly authorized it.
